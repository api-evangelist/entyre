As people age, managing blood pressure becomes an increasingly important part of maintaining overall health. Hypertension, also known as high blood pressure, affects many older adults and is a leading risk factor for heart disease, stroke, kidney disease, and other serious health conditions. Because hypertension often develops without obvious symptoms, regular monitoring and early treatment are essential.
