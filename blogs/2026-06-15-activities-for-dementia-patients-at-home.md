Caring for a spouse or family member with dementia can make everyday life feel unpredictable. As memory, communication, and independence change, many caregivers struggle to find meaningful ways to spend time together. The right activities for dementia patients at home can provide structure, comfort, and connection while helping your loved one stay engaged in daily life.
