No family wants to face a medical crisis without knowing what their loved one would want. Yet many people never document their end-of-life wishes, leaving family caregivers to make difficult decisions under pressure. Planning ahead can provide clarity, reduce stress, and ensure healthcare decisions reflect a person’s values and preferences.
